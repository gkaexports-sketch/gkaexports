About Us

From Our Farmers To Your Market.

Gajanan Kela Agency is based in Khirward, Taluka Raver, District Jalgaon, Maharashtra.

We procure raw green bananas from local farmers and coordinate harvesting, labour, transportation, cutting, washing and packing.

The prepared produce is then supplied to dealers and customers outside Maharashtra.

Our Process

From Harvest To Market.

We coordinate every important stage of the supply process.

01 / FARM

Farmer Procurement

Green bananas are sourced through our farmer network.

02 / HARVEST

Harvesting

Harvesting and labour requirements are coordinated.

03 / TRANSPORT

Transportation

Produce is transported for processing and packing.

04 / PREPARE

Cut & Wash

Bananas are cut and washed before the packing stage.

05 / PACK

Packing

Produce is prepared and packed for transportation.

06 / SUPPLY

Dealer Supply

Packed produce is supplied to dealers and customers.

07 / MARKET

Market Reach

Our supply network reaches markets outside Maharashtra.
